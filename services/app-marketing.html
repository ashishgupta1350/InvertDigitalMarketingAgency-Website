Campaign types we run

How we work

Five steps from first install to active users.

1

Audit & goal

We agree what a good user looks like: an install, a sign-up, a first transaction.

2

Measure first

Play Console, MMP and in-app events are set up and tested before spend starts.

3

Test small

A modest budget across a few creatives and audiences to find what installs cheaply.

4

Move to events

Once data builds up, campaigns optimise toward sign-ups or purchases, not installs alone.

5

Scale & review

Weekly reviews of cost per install, cost per action and retention. Budget follows quality.
