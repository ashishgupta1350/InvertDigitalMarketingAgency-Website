Campaign types we run

Four ways to use video that earns its budget.

YouTube is managed through Google Ads, so it shares your conversion tracking and audiences with your Search campaigns.

  • In-stream awareness: introduce a product or a problem to a defined audience before they start searching.
  • Shorts: short vertical video for reach at a low cost per view, with a clear call to action at the end.
  • Demand Gen: visual formats that push people towards a form, call or product page.
  • Viewer remarketing: a second ad for people who watched most of the first one, with a stronger offer.

Video creative

What a good YouTube ad actually looks like.

Most YouTube budgets are lost in the first five seconds. This is the brief we work from.

What usually fails

  • Opening with a logo, an intro or "Hello, we are"
  • A 90-second brand film with no clear next step
  • One video shown to everyone, whatever their stage
  • Counting views as the result

What we brief

  • A hook in the first five seconds that names the viewer's problem
  • One idea per video, with the product or outcome shown on screen
  • Captions on, because many people watch without sound
  • A single, spoken and on-screen call to action
  • Separate cuts for in-stream, Shorts and remarketing
Who it's for

When YouTube is worth adding to the mix.

We do not have a published YouTube case study yet, so here is a straight answer on fit instead.

Good fit

  • You already run Search and want more people to know you before they search
  • Your product is easier to show than to describe, such as interiors, equipment or courses
  • The buying decision is considered and takes time
  • You can produce a simple video, even on a phone

Start elsewhere if

  • You have no tracking yet, so video's contribution could not be measured
  • You need leads this week from a small budget, where Google Search is faster
  • You cannot follow up with the people who watch
