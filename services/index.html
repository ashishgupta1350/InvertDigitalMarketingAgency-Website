How to choose a channel

Capture demand, or create it.

Every ad channel does one of two jobs. Knowing which one your business needs saves months of guesswork.

Intent-capture channels

People are already searching for what you sell. You show up at that moment and win the enquiry.

Google SearchMicrosoft AdsShoppingCall ads
  • Best when buyers type "manufacturer near me", "price" or "supplier"
  • Leads usually arrive in the first week
  • Volume is capped by how many people search

Start with Google Ads

Demand-creation channels

Few people search for you yet. You reach them while they scroll, watch or work, and give them a reason to enquire.

Meta AdsYouTubeLinkedInRedditSnapchat
  • Best for admissions, interiors, real estate and B2B services
  • Creative and offer decide who responds
  • Scales further, but needs sharper lead qualification

Start with Meta Ads
