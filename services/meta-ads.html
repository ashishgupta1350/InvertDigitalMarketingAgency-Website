Campaign types we run

Pick the format that matches how your buyers reply.

The best format depends on whether your buyers prefer to fill a form, chat on WhatsApp or read a page first.

  • Instant lead forms: the fastest route to an enquiry, with qualifying questions to filter out casual taps.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp: for businesses that close sales in chat, from interiors to local services.
  • Traffic to a landing page: optimised for a conversion event on your own page, with Pixel and CAPI tracking.
  • Video, Reels and Stories: short vertical video that shows the product, the space or the outcome.
  • Retargeting: reminders for people who watched your video or visited your page.
  • Advantage+ campaigns: automated audience and placement selection, tested against manual set-ups.
From the Aggarwal College campaign

One admission season, two audiences

The client had no creative assets and lead forms only. We wrote separate hooks for the two people involved in the decision.

Parents

Safety, career outcomes and the college's reputation.

Students

Course content, campus life and what a law degree opens up.

We tested video and images side by side. Video won, and the best video ads ran at about ₹50 per lead.

Our approach

What makes Meta leads worth calling.

Cheap leads are easy on Meta. Good leads take a few deliberate choices.

A little friction, on purpose

Lead forms that ask one or two qualifying questions cost slightly more per lead, but save your team from calling people who never meant to enquire.

Creative does the targeting

On Meta, the ad itself decides who responds. We brief a different hook for each decision maker and each property type, course or product line.

Tracking that survives privacy changes

Pixel alone loses events. We add the Conversions API and check event matching in Events Manager, so campaigns keep optimising on real conversions.

New campaigns as batches fill

For seasonal businesses such as admissions, we open and close campaigns as seats fill, instead of letting one tired campaign run on.

Meta Ads FAQ

Questions about Meta Ads.

If people already search for what you sell, Google Search usually comes first. If you need to create demand, as with admissions, interiors or real estate, Meta tends to work better. Many accounts use both, and the audit will tell you where to start.

We add qualifying questions to lead forms, brief creative that speaks to the right buyer, and review lead quality with your team every month. You see the same lead sheet we do.

The Conversions API sends conversion events from your server to Meta, alongside the browser Pixel. It fills gaps caused by ad blockers and privacy settings, so Meta optimises on more of your real leads. We recommend it for every account.

Lead forms are best when you want a name and phone number to call back. Click-to-WhatsApp suits businesses that close in chat and can reply within minutes. We often test both against each other for the first few weeks.

It helps, but it is not essential. Our college client came with no creative assets. We brief the scripts and shots, and work with whatever photos or phone footage you can capture.

Meta needs enough daily spend to leave the learning phase. For most businesses, ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 a month in ad spend is a sensible start. Our college campaign produced 500+ leads on about ₹45,000 in total. You pay Meta directly.
