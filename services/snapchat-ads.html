What's included

Snapchat campaigns, built end to end.

From creative to tracking, we handle the parts that decide whether Snapchat is a cheap test or an expensive mistake.

Snap Ads

Full-screen vertical video that appears between Stories and content. We design for the first two seconds, since that is all you get before a swipe.

Story ads

Branded tiles in the Discover feed that open into a set of Snaps. Useful for explaining a product or offer in a few frames.

AR Lenses

Interactive lenses people can try and share. Good for D2C and beauty, fashion and entertainment brands that want engagement, not just views.

App install campaigns

Install campaigns with deep links into the app, tied to your attribution tool so we can see installs and in-app actions, not installs alone.

Snap Pixel and Conversions API

Pixel and server-side Conversions API for leads, sign-ups and purchases, with deduplication so events are not counted twice.

Audience and creative testing

Age, interest, lookalike and retargeting audiences tested in separate ad squads, with several creatives per audience so we learn what works.

Who it's for

Snapchat works best for a few types of business.

We have not published a Snapchat case study yet, so here is an honest view of where it tends to fit.

D2C and consumer brands

Fashion, beauty, food, fitness and gadgets aimed at a young audience, especially where a visual demo helps.

Education and coaching

Courses, test prep and study-abroad counselling aimed at students and young professionals. Best with a quick lead form or WhatsApp step.

Apps and games

Install campaigns for consumer apps where the audience is young and mobile-first.

When it is not a fit

B2B, high-ticket services and older audiences are usually better served by Google Search or LinkedIn. We will tell you in the audit.

Snapchat is best added to a mix once your tracking and landing page are working. We would suggest a modest, time-boxed test and judge it on cost per lead or install.
