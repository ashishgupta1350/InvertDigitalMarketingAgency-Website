Campaign types we run

Keep it simple, keep it search.

As with Google, we begin with high-intent Search and add other formats only when the data supports them.

  • Search campaigns: high-intent keywords on Bing and partner sites, mirrored from your best Google ad groups.
  • Imported Google campaigns: a fast way to launch, followed by a manual clean-up of settings and bids.
  • Audience targeting: LinkedIn profile targeting and in-market audiences on top of search keywords.
  • Shopping and remarketing: product ads and retargeting for catalogue and repeat-purchase businesses.
Why it can be cheaper

A smaller auction, a different crowd

Fewer advertisers compete on Microsoft, which often means lower CPCs for the same keyword. The audience also skews towards desktop users, older professionals and people on Windows and Edge, which suits many B2B and service businesses.

Volume is smaller than Google, so expect fewer leads in total. The goal is extra leads at a lower cost, not a replacement for Google.

Who it's for

Is Microsoft Ads right for you?

We have no published Microsoft Ads case study yet, so this is general guidance, not a promise.

Worth testing if

  • Google Search is already working and you want more volume at a similar or lower cost
  • You sell to professionals or businesses, often researching on a work desktop
  • Google CPCs in your market are high
  • You want to reach company and job-function audiences through search

Not yet if

  • Google Search is not tracked or profitable, since Microsoft will inherit the same problems
  • Your buyers are mostly young and mobile-first
  • Your budget is too small to run two search accounts properly
Honest expectations

What to expect from Microsoft Ads.

Smaller volume

Fewer people search on Bing than on Google. Treat it as an add-on that fills in demand Google misses.

Different behaviour

A keyword that converts on Google may behave differently here. We test it rather than assuming it will copy across.

Quick to launch

Because of the import tool, a Microsoft account can be live within days, once tracking and settings are checked.
