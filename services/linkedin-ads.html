What's included

LinkedIn campaigns built for a small, valuable audience.

Lead Gen Forms

In-platform forms pre-filled from the member's profile, so people can enquire without leaving LinkedIn. We keep them short and test the offer behind them.

Sponsored Content

Single-image, video and document ads in the feed. We lead with a specific problem your buyer recognises, not a company introduction.

Thought Leader Ads

Promote a post from a founder or expert, so the ad arrives as a real person's point of view. Often a good fit for founder-led firms.

Campaign types we run

Start narrow, then widen what works.

LinkedIn audiences can be small. We would rather test a tight audience properly than spread budget thin.

  • Lead generation: Lead Gen Forms attached to a guide, checklist, event or consultation offer.
  • Account-based campaigns: matched company lists with ads written for the specific roles inside each account.
  • Founder and expert content: Thought Leader Ads built from posts that already earn attention.
  • Retargeting: website visitors and video viewers who have already seen your name.
  • Event and webinar invites: Conversation ads and forms that pre-fill a registration.

Who it's for

An honest guide to when LinkedIn is worth it.

We have not published a LinkedIn case study yet, so we will not make one up. What we can do is tell you plainly whether LinkedIn suits your business.

LinkedIn is a good fit if

  • You sell to businesses, and one customer is worth a lot over time
  • Your buyer has a clear job title or sits in a defined industry
  • You offer B2B services, SaaS, training, consulting or professional services
  • You have a content asset, such as a guide, webinar or case study, worth a click
  • Your sales team can follow up within a day

Look at other channels first if

  • Buyers are already searching for you, in which case Google Search is usually cheaper
  • You sell low-ticket products to consumers
  • Your total monthly budget is very small, since LinkedIn typically costs more per lead
  • You have no way to tell good leads from poor ones yet
Our approach

How we make LinkedIn earn its place.

Pair it with Search

LinkedIn creates attention. Google Search catches the people who then look you up. Running both usually beats either alone.

Judge by lead quality

Cost per lead means little if the leads are students or job seekers. We review who actually came through with your sales team every month.

Allow for a longer cycle

B2B decisions take weeks. We track the full path, not only the first form fill, so a slow lead is not mistaken for a bad one.
