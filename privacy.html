Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Invert Digital ("we", "us") is a small performance marketing agency based in Rohini, Delhi, India. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you fill in a form on this website, we collect the details you choose to give us:

  • Name
  • Phone number or WhatsApp number
  • Email address (on the contact form)
  • Business name or website
  • The service you are interested in and your approximate monthly ad budget
  • Any message you write to us

If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, we also hold whatever you send us in those messages.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, prepare the audit or proposal you asked for and follow up about it. We do not use them for anything unrelated.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are received through Netlify Forms and forwarded to us by email. They are stored in Netlify's systems and in our email inbox. We keep enquiry details only for as long as they are needed to deal with your request or manage any resulting work with you.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and which buttons are clicked. If we enable the Meta Pixel, it is used to measure the performance of our own advertising. These tools set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a browser extension that opts you out of tracking.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the website and handle your enquiry (such as Netlify, Google and our email provider), and where the law requires us to.

Your rights and deletion requests

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have rights over your personal data, including the right to ask what we hold, to have it corrected and to have it erased. To make a request, email us at ashishgupta1350@gmail.com from the address or number you used, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Third-party links

Our site links to services such as WhatsApp. When you use them, their own privacy policies apply, and we have no control over how they handle your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.
