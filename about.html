The story

Started from the ads, not the pitch deck.

I'm Ashish Gupta, the founder of Invert Digital. Before starting Invert, I co-owned and ran campaigns at a digital agency in Delhi NCR. I worked inside the ad accounts every week: building keyword lists, reading search terms, setting up call tracking and rewriting landing pages.

That work is where the case studies on this site come from. A Faridabad pipe manufacturer on ₹500 a day, a US law firm paying for junk Display clicks, a South Delhi institute that needed enquiries as organic traffic shrank. Each one taught me the same thing: results come from tracking, patience and weekly attention, not from a bigger retainer.

I started Invert to do that work directly with business owners, without layers in between. You will speak to the person who actually runs your ads.

What I do hands-on

  • Google Ads: Search, Performance Max, Shopping and call campaigns
  • Meta Ads: lead forms, click-to-WhatsApp and video creative
  • GA4 and Google Tag Manager setup
  • CallRail call tracking and lead-sheet reconciliation
  • Landing pages built for ad traffic
Why "Invert"

We invert the usual model: start from the lead.

Most agencies begin with impressions and clicks and hope leads follow. We begin with the lead you want, how many, from whom, at what cost, and work backwards to the keywords, audiences, pages and tracking that produce it.

The usual way

  • Budget
  • Ads and reach
  • Clicks
  • Hope for leads

The Invert way

  • The lead you want
  • The page and tracking that capture it
  • The keywords and audiences that bring it
  • The budget needed to get there
